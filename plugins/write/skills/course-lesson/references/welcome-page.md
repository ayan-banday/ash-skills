# Welcome Pages and Other Student-Facing Pages

Contents: when to use this, the page Ash approved, what it shows, the rules that differ from a daily module, and facts to confirm.

A welcome page, week intro, or any page that is not a daily module takes its structure from Ash. He decides the sections, their order, and what each one says. This skill supplies the voice, the sentence rules, and the formatting. Never impose the module skeleton on these pages, and never add a template such as "Why this matters / Mistakes / Do this". Ash rejected that because it reads as repetitive.

## The approved welcome page

This is the Agriculture Edition welcome page as Ash approved it. Match its feel, not its words.

# Welcome to From Idea to Business: Agriculture Edition

Hi, and welcome.

You're probably reading this between an exam and an assignment.

**Your week is already full:**

- **Exams** you need to study for
- **Projects and assignments** with deadlines
- **Friends, family, and partners** who want your time

And you still want to enjoy college while you're here.

So how do you learn entrepreneurship on top of all that? This course is built for exactly that.

## Who is this for?

You signed up for Udyaan, and you may not be sure what you're getting into. That's okay.

- Maybe you **like entrepreneurship** and have **no clue how to turn an idea into something customers will pay for.**
- Maybe you **wonder if an idea is even worth building.**
- Maybe **your family is in agriculture** and you know very little about it yourself.
- Maybe you're **not sure you want an agriculture business at all.**

What you do know is that you've got an **itch to build something**, even if you can't say yet what that means.

## How this course helps

You don't need an idea to start. Over six weeks, you will:

- **Understand what running a business actually involves**, so it stops feeling confusing or scary
- **Spot problems worth solving**, so you don't spend months building something nobody needs
- **Find real customers** and test your idea with them
- **Leave with a clear next step** and know where to get support

## What your week looks like

- **3 to 5 modules a week**
- **20 to 40 minutes each**
- **Videos under 7 minutes**
- **Pages you can read in about 5 minutes**

You'll be busy, and sometimes sick. The course expects that. Miss a day and you can catch up on the weekend. **Nothing expires.**

(If you have a question, send it to me. I'll answer it personally.)

## What it shows

- **It opens in the reader's moment.** "Between an exam and an assignment" puts the student in the page before any claim is made.
- **Private doubts sit in "Maybe you..." lines,** with the key phrase bold.
- **Every promise is plain.** "Understand what running a business actually involves, so it stops feeling confusing or scary" names the result and the feeling it removes.
- **The author appears once,** in a short block: role, a nerd line, credibility, one joke. Credentials never lead.
- **The page ends on one next step,** with the next module named and how long it takes.

## Rules that differ from a daily module

- **One quiet offer in brackets is allowed,** like "(If you have a question, send it to me. I'll answer it personally.)". Daily modules have no bracketed asides.
- **Headings may be plain questions** where they sound natural ("Who is this for?", "Who am I?").
- **There is no Do, week map, or tomorrow section.** The ending is one next step.
- **A paragraph may run to four sentences** when the sentences are short, as in "You'll be busy, and sometimes sick." The linter allows four in page mode.
- **The author block follows this order:** role, a nerd line, credentials, then one light joke. Keep curses, war or military metaphors, and personal crisis stories out of the whole page.

## Facts to confirm with Ash

Credentials and numbers come from Ash. Do not carry them over from this sample. In particular:

- The country count in the author block has appeared as both 25+ and 50 across drafts. Ask which is current.
- "Maybe even get funding for your startup" appeared in an early draft and not in the approved page. Include it only if Ash names a real route.
- Module length and video length come from the course spec. The spec has said videos under 5 minutes while the page says under 7, so check before repeating either.
