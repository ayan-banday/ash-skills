# Facts (fictional sample brief)
- Coastline Cold Chain drives fresh fish from Kochi harbour to restaurants and hotels in Bengaluru, Chennai and Coimbatore.
- 38 refrigerated trucks. Drivers log temperatures on paper at each stop.
- Last summer some buyers turned away loads they said arrived warm.
- What the client said at the meeting in Kochi (verbatim): "We lose fish we can't see going bad." / "Drivers write temperatures on paper, and nobody reads it." / "When a buyer complains, we can't prove what happened."
- Tidewater Studio (Kochi) has four teams: Sensors (fits and looks after sensors), App (builds the driver tablet and the office screen), Data (keeps a record of every trip), Field (trains drivers on their own routes).
- The offer: two sensors per truck (inside the box, on the door) that report through the night; a driver tablet in place of the paper log; an office screen; a temperature record for every load.
- The pilot: one route. Contact: Meera Nair, project lead, meera.nair@tidewater.example. Reply with a route; within a week she sends a one-page plan for that route, including what the pilot costs.
- Example night (labelled as an example): leaves Kochi 6 pm; stops at Coimbatore 10 pm, Salem 2 am, Hosur 5 am; Bengaluru 7 am.
