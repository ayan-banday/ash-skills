What does the motorbike stand for?

We drew TVS as one of its own motorbikes. The frame is the TVS Way, how TVS already works. Each of the four parts is one thing you asked JAIN for at Attibele.

Handlebar: your leaders

How do we help experts lead people?

TVS promotes experts for their skill, then asks them to lead a team. JAIN runs management programmes built on TVS's own cases.

What TVS gets
Hours that count toward a degree
A PG Diploma or Executive MBA from JAIN, a NAAC A++ university.

Engine: your suppliers

How do we see what slows a supplier down?

Small supplier plants (MSMEs) make the parts inside every TVS bike. JAIN interviews each owner and their line leads, and writes down what is slowing them.

What TVS gets
A short brief on each supplier
Ready before your next supplier review.

Dashboard: your systems

How do we spot a stopped machine right away?

Many line checks still happen on paper, so a stopped machine is found late. JAIN students fit sensors and build tablet forms on your live projects.

Body: design

How do we make new ideas easy to show and teach?

JAIN's design school sketches new accessories and turns your methods into short videos.

What TVS gets
Apps people use without training
Simple screens for the shop floor.

Frame: the TVS Way

How do we pass on the TVS Way?

The TVS way of working lives in senior people's heads, so it is hard to teach. JAIN's centre for Indian Knowledge Systems helps turn it into lessons your Dojo can use.

What TVS gets
A master's skill that stays
A senior teaches a few new hires side by side, the guru-shishya way.
