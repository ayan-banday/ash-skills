EXAMPLE NIGHT 01 / 05

6 PM, KOCHI HARBOUR

The load leaves Kochi at 6 pm. From here to Bengaluru, nobody can see inside the box.

2 AM, THE SALEM STOP

At the 2 am stop the door stays open too long. The crate nearest the door warms up, and nobody knows.

THE FIX FOR THE BOX: TWO SENSORS

Our Sensors team fits two small sensors: one inside the box, one on the door.

Sensors

THE FIX FOR THE BOX: YOUR OFFICE SEES IT

Each sensor reports through the night. When a crate warms, it shows on your office screen while the truck is still on the road.

THE CAB

Put the logbook on a screen

YOU TOLD US

“Drivers write temperatures on paper, and nobody reads it.”

The sensors report on their own. The tablet replaces the paper log your drivers already keep: at each stop they tap the reading instead of writing it, and the office sees it the same minute.

The goal: a warm reading gets noticed the next morning the same minute.
