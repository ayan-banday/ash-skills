# Handoff: what each host skill does with the board

This skill plans; another skill builds. The board travels as `work/storyboard.md` next to the host's own files. The
host keeps its rules for copy, palette, layout and QA, and adds the story's: one row, one screen, one bright thing,
one mover, the board's words.

## What every host does

1. **Read the board's Thesis, World, Verb and Thread lines** and put the thesis in the first screen and the last.
2. **One row becomes one screen, step or figure.** Do not merge rows, and do not add screens the board lacks; change
   the board first, rerun the checker, then build.
3. **Bright is the focal point.** Make it the brightest, largest or only sharp thing on that screen.
4. **Moves is the only motion.** Everything else is still. In a static host, Moves becomes the difference between
   one figure and the next.
5. **Words are the copy, as written.** The host's writing rules may tighten them, never lengthen them past 20.
6. **Shot sizes become framing:** extreme wide and wide show the whole world small; medium shows one part with its
   surroundings; close-up and tighter fill the frame with the part.
7. **The last screen is the first, changed.** Same framing, every part shown, the thesis back.
8. **Run the covered tests on the render** and the story reviewer (`review.md`) after the host's own QA.

## pitch-site-udyaan

- **The page order still comes from the reader's asks** (`../pitch-site-udyaan/references/story.md`). Each ask is a run of rows with
  its own small spine: obstacle (THE PROBLEM), action (what we put in place), outcome (WHAT [CLIENT] GETS), reaction
  (the motif one stage further on).
- **The thread is the motif.** It builds down the page and returns finished at the close with the ask inside it.
- **Pinned stages** take one row per scroll step; the stage counter matches the row number.
- **An abstract section** (values, a framework, a mental model) becomes catalogue entry ARC-33, built with the dive
  mechanics in `living-metaphors.md` and `assets/examples/range-src/`.
- The proposal's own rules win on facts: quotes verbatim, every number from facts.md, examples labelled.

## brief-page-udyaan

- **A section is a row, or a run of rows inside a scroll story.** The section's one drawn thing is the row's Bright.
- **Motion is light:** where the board says something moves, show the before and after or a short stepped change;
  everything stays readable at rest.
- **Headings still state the answer.** The row's Words feed the heading and its one short paragraph.
- **The finished picture** (the reaction row) is drawn once, whole, as the section's closing figure.

## polished-doc

- **No motion.** Each row is a figure: a stick-figure scene, a story strip panel, a contrast pair. Moves becomes what
  differs between panels.
- **One figure per row, in order,** with the row's Words as the line under it, written as meaning, never as a
  caption that describes the drawing.
- **Open and close on the same scene,** the second time complete.
- Keep the document's structure; the story lives in the figures, not in new sections.

## course-lesson-udyaan

- **The student is the character.** Want: what they are trying to do today. Obstacle: the mistake most students make.
  Action: the rule. Outcome: the good pick. Reaction: the student doing the Do.
- **The labelled mini-examples are the rows.** Good and bad picks become two frames of a contrast pair.
- The lesson skeleton stays; the board orders the examples and decides which one gets a drawing.

## headline-angles-substack

- **Only the opening rules apply:** context, a question the reader now needs answered, and a feeling; start at the
  strongest point, not the general one (`film-craft.md`, The opening). Use them to rank the angles' first lines.

## Slides and anything else

- **One row, one slide.** The Bright is the slide's only image or the highlighted part of it; the Words are the slide.
- **A deck still opens and closes on the same picture.**
- For any new host: map the eight steps in "What every host does" onto it and write the mapping into this file.
