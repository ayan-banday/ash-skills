# Ash's taste in stories

His verdicts on visual stories, in his words. Add to it whenever he reacts. For page design taste (palettes, type,
motion house style) see `../pitch-site/references/taste.md`.

## What he loves

- **The philosophy, not the picture.** "The point is to get the philosophy behind it: that we can use a visual
  metaphor to express information instead of having to write information."
- **Density through a metaphor.** "A visual metaphor can encompass so much detail."
- **The dive.** "I love the part where we can have a deep type thing like that": diving into identity, values,
  curiosity with "sophisticated animation styles". He sees these as mental models.
- **Lights that hide things.** "I love that they are hidden things."
- **The horizontal painting with the climber** walking underneath as the progress bar.
- **Being shown, in order.** He wants it "fed down the throat", never a choose-your-own-adventure.
- **Creative and clear at once.** "Creative as fuck. Yet, still being clear." Clarity wins a tie.

## What he rejects

One list for every skill, in Ash's words: "What Ash rejects" in `../../match-ash-design/references/design.md`.

## How he works on stories

- He dictates by voice; names and words arrive garbled ("Vishva story" for visual story). Confirm from written
  sources.
- Plan first, then a small prototype he can react to, then the full build. Show him the board before the build.
- He studies references (films, video essays, award sites) and wants them distilled into reusable rules with good
  and bad examples.
- He wants every skill to be able to use this one.
