# The Range: source for the living-metaphor dive

The Mauna Kea panel from The Range (2026-10-19), Ash's personal map of his growth, built on the `horizontal` shell.
Copy the mechanics, never the words: the quotes are Ash's own and must not appear in client work.

- `frag-11-maunakea.html`: **the model is everything below the waterline.** The dive: a tall scene (1600 × 1800)
  seen through a moving `viewBox`, the bathysphere and depth gauge that travel with the camera, creatures that wait
  dim until the searchlight finds them, and words written beside each one. See `dive()`, `cam()` and the `LAY` array.
  **The summit above the waterline is the counter-example:** eight numbered stops scattered over the scene, each
  writing words into the sky. Ash found it "clicky", wordy and "not that creative".
- `frag-01-engine.html`: shared helpers. `write()` writes words into an SVG one at a time; `initPanel()` runs the
  numbered stops and the route control; `initLamps()` runs the fixed lamps that reveal hidden extras at night.
- `starter.html`: tokens, `penDraw()`, `rng()`, the night switch.

Method, test and casting bank: `references/living-metaphors.md`; the dive as a storyboard: `references/storyboard.md`.
In pitch-site it is catalogue entry ARC-33.
