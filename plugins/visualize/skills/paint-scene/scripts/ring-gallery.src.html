Ring Eyes: the cast and every format

Pick by name. A character is Ring.svg("fuji",{expr,pose}); a piece is Ring.piece("#el",{format, bg, words, cast}). Small pictures under each character are its 24px thumbnail.

The cast

Expressions

All in the eyes, plus a dot mouth where a character has one. expr: open (default), closed, happy, side, surprised, worried, sleepy, wink.

Poses

Limbs are opt-in: use a pose only when the action is the message. pose: still (default), stand, walk, wave, cheer, point, hold, shrug, sit, hop.
