Every part, by name

Pick parts by these names. Masks and tiles tint to any colour; figures and props come out in any ink. Names in bold are what you type.

Blooms

Watercolour masks painted once with p5.brush. One per screen. CSS: data-bloom="round". Scene: bloom:{name:"round"}.

Paper

Grain and three hatching tiles. Print uses only these.

Light, four kinds

One kind per screen, aimed at the one message (here, the ball). steps is the default; contours only for one hero.

Figures

Face right; {dir:-1} flips. Anchors in brackets: aim the ball or the light at them, as "id.hand".
