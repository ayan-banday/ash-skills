# A study that runs on its own

Ash starts it with "study: <question>", usually from his phone. Saying it is his yes.

## Steps

1. **Mini-grill: 1 or 2 questions, each with a suggestion.** Usually: "What's the hypothesis? Suggestion: <your guess from the question>" and "What would you do differently if it holds? Suggestion: <guess>". Skip what he already said. Done when the hypothesis is one sentence.
2. **Tell him once that it's running,** in one line, and stop asking.
3. **Run it alone.** In Fuji, a study is a research run: follow Shasta's pipeline (`_rules/Taking In Sources.md`, "A research run"): cheap agents scout, fetch and index; the main chat reads only indexes and findings. Stay inside the sources and channels he named; anything outside waits.
4. **Make the phone page.** Read his taste library first (see SKILL.md, "Taste"). Publish it as a private claude.ai artifact. On one phone screen:
   - the hypothesis, in his words
   - **the verdict first:** holds / doesn't hold / not sure yet
   - three reasons, one line each
   - one next step
   - sources behind a tap
5. **Notify him** with the page link: a push notification if the tool exists in the session, else the final message (the Claude app notifies him). The message is one line: "<verdict>: <hypothesis>. Open link."
6. **Save** in Fuji: the finding to the ticket's Answer (or the map's research folder), the log line, then commit.

Bigger studies, or ones that need a person, go to a teammate as a brief (`/visualize:brief-page`), not a run.
