# The critic

The builder reads past its own gaps because it knows the whole page. A fresh agent reads it as Ash, once.

Dispatch with the Agent tool, model `sonnet`, fresh context. Give it the page path, the path of `writing-rules.md` and this prompt. Never this chat or the source material.

> You are reading a study page written for Ash: 20, a BBA student at Jain University who wants to build a technology company. He reads in short bursts, wants every idea from what he knows to its name, and hates having to remember earlier sections to follow a later one.
>
> Read `<writing-rules.md>` first. Then strip the page at `<page.html>` to text with a short script and read it one section at a time, holding back "The answer" until you have written your own.
>
> Report, under 450 words, findings only:
> 1. **Your own answer to the page's title question** in three sentences, written before reading "The answer". Name any line in the page's answer that the page never taught.
> 2. **Every place a section leans on an earlier one** without naming it and repeating the facts (rule 1). Quote it.
> 3. **Every heading, question or "Your turn" that fails read alone** (rule 2). Quote it and give the self-contained version.
> 4. **Every term used before its plain meaning,** or never explained. Quote the line.
> 5. **Every sum in the worked examples and answers:** recompute each one. List any that are wrong.
> 6. **Every idea box without a mini example inside it,** and any metaphor that makes the idea harder.
> 7. **The fixes, ranked, at most eight,** each as the exact old line and the new line.
> No praise. Don't rewrite the page.
