# The look

Ash liked the first book's look: "I love the colours, I love how things are. They can be a little simpler." Keep it.

- **Shasta's palettes** (`_reference/Study Page Palettes.md`): a near-neutral dark page, near-white headings, muted colour only where it does a job. Ink and Clay for tools and systems. No blue, no green. Body text at 7:1.
- **Serif body, sans for labels and tables, hand lettering on paper-board pictures.** Bold key phrases in the accent; one marker highlight per block at most.
- **A little of pitch-site's flair, where it carries the message:** the mind map's branches draw in, a sticky part list at the top, "The answer" as a quiet raised block. One moving thing per screen; everything readable at rest and under reduced motion.
- **Pictures only where they explain faster than words.** The six-step journey picture in the first version was judged garbage because its labels were jargon and it read B to A. A picture's labels follow the same rule as the text: what happens, in one to three plain words.
- **The problem map is drawn, never listed:** the outcome at the top, groups below, arrows for what waits on what, the ideas on the branches.
- **Vary a little between chapters** (the picture, the shape of the answer block, the section rhythm) so the book doesn't read as one template. Clarity wins over variety.
- **Check at desktop width only.** Load the page in Playwright at 1280 px, screenshot the map, the shelves and the chapter picture, and look at them. No phone checks; Ash doesn't study on a phone.
- **Save the HTML in Shasta** under `<Area>/books/`, publish it as a private artifact, and keep the link in the Fuji ticket.
