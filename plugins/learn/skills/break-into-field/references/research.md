# Research, cheap

The aim is how the field's experts think: philosophies, mental models, first principles and the reasoning behind them. Not tools, never syntax.

## Steps

1. **Write a brief for the chapter** before any reading: the reader, the chapter's problem, four to six numbered questions the chapter needs answered, and the output format. One paragraph each. The brief is what every reader agent gets.
2. **Pick the best source per question,** never a ranked list of people. Some people are best for philosophy (for AI: Karpathy, Matt Pocock), some for the practical side (Greg Isenberg for sites), some for the basics (CS50), some for product thinking (Lenny). Ask: who has thought hardest about this exact question?
3. **Text first.** Lecture notes, essays, transcript pages and posts cost less than video and need no transcript fetch. YouTube direct download is often blocked in cloud sessions (bot check); when it is, don't guess what a video says. Search for a text transcript, or move to the next best source.
4. **One cheap reader per angle, not per person.** Each Haiku agent gets the brief, one angle (history, philosophy, agents, practice) and its sources, and returns findings in this shape: the question number, the idea in one plain line, the exact quote, one line of context, the source. No summaries. Giving each reader a different angle is how an idea is paid for once.
5. **Later readers report only what's new** against the findings so far, when the same question is read again.
6. **Open a full transcript only where a finding is thin** and the chapter can't do without it.
7. **Save the findings** to Fuji, `ranges/self/build-fuji/research/<ticket>-<field>-sources.md`, so the next chapter reuses them. Grow the file; never re-read a source already in it.

## What the chapter takes from the findings

- The plain idea and its reasoning, rewritten in Ash's words. The quote stays in the research file, not in the chapter, unless it is the clearest sentence available.
- The source goes under the book's Sources, nowhere else.
